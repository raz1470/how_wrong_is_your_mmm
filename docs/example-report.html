4 · Channel sensitivity

Which channels need more randomness?

This is what decides the lever on page 1. For each channel, how much reliability keeps improving as you widen the amplitude, all the way through to Blackout, with every other channel left untouched. A curve that flattens early means that channel doesn't need much perturbation. A curve still climbing at the far right means the channel is more tightly locked to the planning cycle, and needs the strongest lever available to break it.
